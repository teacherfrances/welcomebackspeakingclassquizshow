The winter break gameshow: know it, say it, use it.

Set up the game

Teams of three or four work best. One person from the team answers, but the whole team can help.

Suggested time gives longer for speaking questions and less for quick ones. You start the timer yourself, so nobody is rushed before they are ready.

How to play

The board

There are five categories and 25 questions. The team whose turn it is chooses any number on the board. Higher numbers are harder.

Scoring

  • Correct: the team wins the full points.
  • Nearly there: half points, for example when the meaning is right but the expression is not quite accurate.
  • Speaking bonus: after every question, the team can win 50 more points by using the expression in a true sentence about their own break.

Whose turn is it?

The turn moves to the next team automatically. To change it, tap a team on the scoreboard.

The timer

Choose your timer before you start, then press Start when the team is ready. If the time runs out, the other teams can try to steal the points.

The big finish

Play this when the board is empty, or any time you are running out of class time. Each team bets some of their points, then speaks for 45 seconds using three expressions from the screen.

Playing on your own

Choose "On my own" at the start. Say your answers out loud, judge yourself honestly, and try to beat 2000 points.

Notes for the teacher

  • Whole game: about 40 to 50 minutes. Half the board plus the big finish: about 25 minutes.
  • Categories 3 and 5 are the real speaking work. Do not rush them, and push for a follow-up question from another team every time.
  • Category 4 revises last term's Australian expressions. Category 1, 2, 3 and 5 use this term's new phrases.
  • Accept any true, clear sentence in the bonus. Correct the form afterwards, not during the speaking.
